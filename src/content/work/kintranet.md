Kintranet is the internal app Kin's sales, service, and underwriting teams use to manage leads, quotes, and policies. It lives in dot-com, Kin's main Rails app. I was one of Kin's first front-end hires in 2019 and made 900+ commits to dot-com, much of it Kintranet work.

## Highlights

- **2019–2020**: coverage details, underwriting rule layouts, renewals, cancellations, and Kinterface, Kin's first component library and docs.
- **2021**: product rules and product configuration pages, and moratorium views.
- **2022–2023**: brought Kinetic into Kintranet and rebuilt Protection Periods with it.
- **2025**: a Protection Period dashboard and role. Protection Periods pause cancellations and nonrenewals for policies in counties under an emergency order, such as after a hurricane.
